# FreeflightArena
MATLAB Code for realtime control of high speed camera, auditory stimulation, and video 3D tracking annotation.

DAQFF monitors HDMI outputs from high speed cameras and controls national instruments NIDAQ to play stimulus and trigger video saving, intended to work with Fastec TS3 series cameras.

sanitizeautosave groups high speed video files into folders, matching the filename conventions of the triggering log generated by DAQFF and expected by AutoSaveValidatory

AutoSaveValidator performs background subtraction and facilitates tracking and annotation of animals in both top and side aspect cameras
